How to find a tool

Start with the task. Check the current QA result before opening another site.

  1. 01

    Search

    Enter the task, or choose a shelf, product kind, or format.

  2. 02

    Compare

    Read the description, price flag, and dated QA state.

  3. 03

    Open

    Visit the selected product at its own sociobot.in address.

What the catalogue does not do

It does not create accounts, store your searches, or hide incomplete QA work.

The optional guide sends only the words you enter to Sociobot. Read the privacy notice.
