Privacy

Know what the catalogue sends and stores

Hello Factory does not create accounts, set cookies, or run analytics.

Catalogue browsing

Pages, catalogue records, and product pictures load from hello-factory.sociobot.in. Search and filters run in your browser.

Optional guide

The guide sends the words you enter to api.sociobot.in only after you choose “Ask the guide”. Do not enter personal or confidential information.

Product links

Opening a listed product leaves this site. That product’s privacy notice applies after you open it.

Questions and requests

Email privacy@sociobot.in for a privacy question or request. There is no Hello Factory account or stored search history to delete.
